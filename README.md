# TCS-FINANCIAL-DASHBORD 3 Page BI
📊 TCS Financial Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive financial performance analysis of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) using Power BI. The dashboard transforms IFRS consolidated financial statement data into interactive visualizations that enable users to evaluate profitability, revenue growth, and financial position over time.

The solution is organized into three analytical pages:

Executive Overview
Income Statement Analysis
Balance Sheet Analysis

The dashboard allows users to explore historical trends, compare key financial metrics, and understand the company's financial performance through interactive slicers and dynamic KPIs.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to convert raw financial statement data into meaningful business insights that help answer questions such as:

How has TCS revenue evolved over time?
Are profitability margins improving or declining?
Which quarter contributes the highest revenue?
How strong is the company's balance sheet?
How have assets, liabilities, equity, and liquidity changed over the years?

📈 Key Business Insights
Executive Overview
Revenue increased steadily over the analysis period, reaching ₹5.34M in 2026.
Gross Margin remained consistently above 40%, reflecting efficient cost management.
Operating Margin stayed stable around 25–28%, indicating operational consistency.
Net Income showed continuous long-term growth alongside revenue expansion.
Q4 generated the highest quarterly revenue among all four quarters.
Income Statement Analysis
Revenue, Gross Profit, and Net Income demonstrated consistent long-term growth.
Gross Profit expanded in line with revenue, indicating scalable operations.
Profitability margins remained relatively stable despite business expansion.
Year-over-Year revenue growth moderated during recent years, suggesting a transition toward a more mature growth phase.
Earnings remained strong with sustained profitability across the reporting period.
Balance Sheet Analysis
Total Assets increased significantly over the analysis period, reflecting business expansion.
Shareholders' Equity remained the primary source of financing, indicating a strong capital structure.
Cash & Cash Equivalents improved over time, strengthening liquidity.
Liabilities increased alongside asset growth while maintaining a balanced financial position.
The company maintained healthy liquidity ratios throughout the analysis period.
